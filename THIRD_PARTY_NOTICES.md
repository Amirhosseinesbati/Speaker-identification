## Spherical PLDA equations

`scripts/evaluate_spherical_plda_known_rerank.py` adapts the EM and scoring
equations from:

- Alexey Sholokhov, Nikita Kuzmin, Kong Aik Lee, and Eng Siong Chng,
  *Probabilistic Back-ends for Online Speaker Recognition and Clustering*,
  ICASSP 2023, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.09523>.
- Reference implementation:
  <https://github.com/sholokhovalexey/online-speaker-clustering>.
